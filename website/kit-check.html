About 10 minutes · five questions · nothing is recorded against you

Read this before you start, because it changes what these questions mean.
This is not a test, and it is not a gate. Nothing here can stop you climbing.

It exists because the thing that actually derails beginners in this course is never quantum physics — it is a radian, or a Python dictionary, arriving without warning in the middle of a sentence about qubits. Then you get stuck, and it feels like you failed at quantum mechanics when really you tripped over a unit.

So: five questions. Get one wrong and a 90-second refresher opens right there. That is the entire mechanism. Wrong answers are the point — they are how the refreshers find you.
One thing you do not need: complex numbers. Some quantum courses demand them on page one. This one deliberately keeps every amplitude real and non-negative, so the Born rule stays plain squaring and you will never meet an i. If that was the thing putting you off, you are clear.
